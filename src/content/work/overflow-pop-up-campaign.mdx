## The brief

Overflow Mobile Coffee Bar needed promotional content for an upcoming pop-up
at Mango Tree in Fonthill, Ontario. The campaign needed to communicate the
location, date, featured drinks, and limited-time flavours while still feeling
playful and consistent with the mobile coffee cart's personality.

## The creative direction

The campaign was built around the idea that **all roads lead to Mango Tree**.

I used a winding pink route graphic to guide the viewer from the event
information toward the featured drinks. This turned the location into part of
the visual concept rather than presenting it as another line of text.

The soft neutral background kept the composition approachable, while the
green, yellow, and pink accents connected the campaign to the Mango Tree
location and Overflow's colourful drink offerings.

## Designing the campaign

**Product-first imagery.** The three drinks were placed prominently at the
bottom of the poster so customers could immediately see what would be
available.

<div class="of-drinks">
  <div class="of-drink"><span class="dot"></span><span class="name">Vanilla iced matcha</span></div>
  <div class="of-drink"><span class="dot"></span><span class="name">Banana bread latte</span></div>
  <div class="of-drink"><span class="dot"></span><span class="name">Classic dirty soda</span></div>
</div>

**Clear information hierarchy.** The location appears first, followed by the
visual route and featured drinks. This made the design easy to scan while
scrolling on social media.

**Social copy with personality.** I paired the poster with an Instagram caption
that listed the special flavours, event date, operating hours, and address in a
friendly and conversational voice.

For a local pop-up campaign, the design has only a few seconds to answer four
questions before someone keeps scrolling:

<div class="of-four">
  <div><span class="ofq">What</span>Overflow Mobile Coffee Bar pop-up</div>
  <div><span class="ofq">Where</span>Mango Tree &middot; Fonthill, Ontario</div>
  <div><span class="ofq">When</span>Date &amp; hours, in the poster</div>
  <div><span class="ofq">Why</span>New flavours, limited time</div>
</div>

## What I learned

This project reinforced that event marketing needs both personality and
precision. The visual concept helps someone stop scrolling, but the hierarchy
and event details are what help them decide to attend.
